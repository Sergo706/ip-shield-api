Welcome to IP Shield. This Privacy Policy explains how IP Shield ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, processes, and protects information when you use our services, websites, and applications.

We are committed to transparency and want you to understand what information we collect, how it is used, and how it is protected.

By accessing or using IP Shield, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you, automatically through your use of our services, and from third-party providers integrated in our platform when you choose to use them.

### Account and Registration Information

When you create an account or sign in to our services, we may collect information such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Authentication credentials
* Account preferences and settings

### Interaction Data

We collect information about how you interact with our services, including:

* Forms you submit
* Features you use
* Preferences you configure
* Customer support communications
* Service-related interactions

### Third-Party Authentication Information

If you choose to sign in using a third-party provider such as Google, GitHub, or another supported authentication service, we may receive profile and authentication information from that provider as permitted by your account settings and the provider's policies.

### Automatic and Telemetry Data

We automatically collect technical, diagnostic, security, and performance-related information, including:

* IP addresses
* Device information
* Browser and operating system information
* Device fingerprinting signals
* Estimated geographic location derived from IP addresses
* Usage statistics and service interactions
* Network and performance telemetry
* Error and diagnostic information

This information helps us understand how our services are used, maintain security, improve performance, and troubleshoot issues.

### Service Delivery and Analytics

We use information to:

* Operate and maintain our services

* Troubleshoot technical issues
* Improve service functionality and user experience

We may use information to:

We analyze usage patterns and feedback to:

* Develop new features

We analyze information collected through our services, including usage metrics, device information, estimated location information, and interaction data, to better understand our user base and improve our products and services.

# Privacy Policy

At IP Shield, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

## Information Collection

When you use the IP Shield API, we collect:
- **Account Information**: Email address and billing details when you create an account.
- **Usage Data**: API request volume, latency metrics, and anonymized query logs for performance monitoring.

## Data Usage

The data we collect is strictly used to:
1. Provide, maintain, and improve our API services.
2. Monitor against abuse, unauthorized access, and DDOS attacks.
3. Process your subscription payments.

We **do not** sell your data or share your query logs with third-party advertisers.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact our support team.
